# Findings

**Scope: *our* results only** — what we computed, ran, or derived. External knowledge (literature, specs, reported leads) lives in `docs/kb/`, not here. The test is provenance of the *claim*: if its proof is a paper, it's kb; if its proof is our data/run, it's a finding. ("Literature finding" is a category error.)

Curated, append-only. Each finding links to its evidence (`Exp N`, or a data artifact). Negative results are equally important — they prevent re-exploring dead ends.
